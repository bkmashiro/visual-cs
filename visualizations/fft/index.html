Pseudocode
FFT(x, N):
  bit_reverse(x)
  for s = 1 to log₂N:
    m ← 2s
    w_m ← e−2πi/m
    for k = 0,m,…,N−m:
      w ← 1
      for j = 0 to m/2−1:
        t ← w · x[k+j+m/2]
        u ← x[k+j]
        x[k+j] ← u + t
        x[k+j+m/2] ← u − t
        w ← w · w_m
About FFT

Cooley-Tukey radix-2 DIT FFT reduces DFT cost from O(N²) to O(N log N) by recursively splitting even/odd indices.


Each butterfly merges two sub-transforms via a twiddle factor W = e−2πik/N — a unit-circle rotation that encodes phase shift.


N=8: brute DFT needs 64 mults; FFT needs only 12 butterfly ops (3 stages × 4 butterflies).
