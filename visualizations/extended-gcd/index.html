🔢 Extended Euclidean Algorithm

Extends the basic Euclidean Algorithm to also compute Bézout coefficients x and y satisfying a·x + b·y = gcd(a,b).

Time: O(log min(a,b)) — same as basic GCD, just tracks two extra sequences.

Applications: Modular inverse (a⁻¹ mod m), Chinese Remainder Theorem, RSA key generation, solving linear Diophantine equations.

Recurrence Relations

After finding all quotients q₀, q₁, …, we track three sequences in parallel:

r_{i+1} = r_{i-1} − q_i · r_i

s_{i+1} = s_{i-1} − q_i · s_i

t_{i+1} = t_{i-1} − q_i · t_i

Initialized: r₀=a, r₁=b / s₀=1, s₁=0 / t₀=0, t₁=1

Invariant maintained: a·sᵢ + b·tᵢ = rᵢ at every step.
